Drug Response Prediction System

Project Overview

  • Goal: Predict cancer drug response (IC50 values) using machine learning.
  • Target Variable: LN(IC50) - Natural logarithm of the drug concentration needed to inhibit 50% of cell growth.
  • Application: To provide personalized cancer treatment recommendations.
