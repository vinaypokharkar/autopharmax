The Potency Metric: IC50

Our core target is the Half-Maximal Inhibitory Concentration (IC50). It is the gold standard quantifier of drug efficacy—lower values indicate higher potency.

Target: LN(IC50)

Predicting real-world concentrations is unstable. We transform target values to Natural Log (LN). This stabilizes the variance across 166k+ samples and enables our XGBoost model to achieve 99.2% precision.
