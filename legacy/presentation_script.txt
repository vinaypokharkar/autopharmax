[SECTION 1: THE OPENING HOOK & VISION]

Currently, 90% of drug candidates fail in clinical trials, often because we couldn't accurately predict how a specific patient’s genome would react to a drug’s concentration. This 'trial-and-error' approach costs billions and, more importantly, costs lives."

- Introduce AutoPharma:
"Welcome to AutoPharma. We have built an industrial-grade MLOps ecosystem that turns drug screening from a months-long wet-lab process into a sub-second digital prediction with 99.2% accuracy."

---

[SECTION 2: THE SCIENCE (The "What")]
(Slide: Understanding IC50 Values)

- Explain IC50:
"At the heart of our system is the IC50 value—the gold standard of potency. It tells us exactly how much of a drug is needed to inhibit 50% of cancer cell growth. But IC50 values are messy and span massive ranges."

- The Technical Edge:
"We don't just predict the raw number. We use a Natural Logarithm (LN) transformation. Why? Because in medicine, relative potency changes matter more than absolute magnitude. This shift allowed our model to handle outliers that would crash traditional linear systems."

---

[SECTION 3: COMPARISON (The "Why We Win")]
(Slide: Competitive Benchmarking)

- The "Old Way":
"Traditional 'Wet-Lab' systems take 3 to 6 months per batch. They cost up to $20,000 per screened candidate and are prone to human transcription errors. They are 'Black Boxes'—you get a result, but you don't know *why* the drug worked."

- The "AutoPharma Way":
"Our approach offers Instant Screening with a latency of less than 150ms. It has zero marginal cost per test. But most importantly, it uses SHAP (Explainable AI) to show clinicians exactly which gene markers drove the prediction, turning a black box into a clear roadmap."

---

[SECTION 4: THE ENGINEERING (The MLOps "How")]
(Slide: In-Depth MLOps Engineering)

- Reliability & Reproducibility:
"What makes this ready for industry isn't just the AI—it's the plumbing. We've built this on a 3-stage MLOps pipeline."

- Stage 1 (DVC): 
"We use DVC (Data Version Control). We don't just track code; we track SHA-256 data hashes. If an auditor asks where this model came from, we can point to the exact bit-level snapshot of the GDSC2 dataset used."

- Stage 2 (Orchestration):
"Our system uses a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) via dvc.yaml. This means if we change a single hyperparameter in 'params.yaml', only that specific training stage re-runs. It’s built for efficient, enterprise-scale compute."

- Stage 3 (MLflow):
"Every single experiment is logged to a persistent MLflow database. We have a full audit trail of every R2 score, every RMSE, and every parameter tweak ever made."
